About the Project

Mind Steps is a website I built to help people learn about Alzheimer's disease and keep their minds active. I wanted to make something that could be useful for both people who are worried about their own memory and for families who are caring for someone with Alzheimer's.

Alzheimer's is the most common form of dementia, and it affects millions of families. A lot of the information online can feel overwhelming, so my goal was to put the basics in one simple, easy-to-read place, along with some games that give your brain a little workout.

Features
Information – What Alzheimer's is, how it changes the brain, and the 10 warning signs
Factors and Lifestyle – Risk factors and healthy habits that may help lower your risk
Support and Care – Tips for daily life, safety at home, and support for caregivers
Math Games – Quick math questions with two levels:
Easy: add and subtract numbers from 1 to 10
Medium: add and subtract numbers from 10 to 20, plus times tables
Alphabet Games – Letter games with two levels:
Easy: look at a letter and say whether it matches
Medium: decide which letter comes next in the alphabet
Memory Check-in – A short set of yes/no questions to help you notice changes in memory or thinking
Resources – Links and phone numbers for trusted organizations like the Alzheimer's Association

Built With
Python and Flask for the backend (pages and game logic)
HTML and CSS for the pages and design
JavaScript for the games and the Memory Check-in
Replit to write and run the code
