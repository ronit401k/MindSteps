Living with Alzheimer’s disease involves changes over time, but with right care, support, and understanding, people with Alzheimer's and caregivers can maintain a good quality of life.

Living With Alzheimer’s Disease

Daily Life and Routine

  • Establishing simple daily routines can help reduce confusion and anxiety.
  • Keeping frequently used items in the same place supports independence.
  • Using calendars, reminders, and labels can help with memory and orientation.
  • Maintaining familiar environments can provide comfort and reassurance.

Emotional and Social Well-being

  • Staying socially connected can help improve mood and emotional health.
  • Engaging in familiar and enjoyable activities supports a sense of purpose.
  • Focusing on abilities rather than limitations helps maintain dignity.

Caregiver Support

Supporting a Loved One

  • Learning about Alzheimer’s disease helps caregivers understand changes in behavior.
  • Clear communication and patience can reduce frustration for both caregiver and patient.
  • Creating a calm and structured environment can help manage daily challenges.

Taking Care of Yourself

  • Caregivers should take regular breaks to protect their physical and mental health.
  • Seeking help from family members, friends, or support groups is important.
  • Community resources such as respite care or adult day programs can provide relief.
  • Reaching out to healthcare professionals can offer guidance and emotional support.

Caregiving can be demanding, and no one should feel they have to do it alone. Support and resources are available to help caregivers navigate this journey. Visit the Resources page
