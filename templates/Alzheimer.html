Understanding Alzheimer's and what you can do

Alzheimer's is a progressive brain disorder that damages the brain, leading to issues in memory, thinking, cognitive capabilities, and even in performing simple tasks, like eating, drinking, or even walking. Let's learn more about it and how we can support someone with Alzheimer's.
