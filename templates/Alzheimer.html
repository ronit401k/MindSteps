7.4 million Americans are living with Alzheimer's

That's about 1 in 9 people age 65 and older, and behind every number is a family. Mind Steps is here to help you learn about Alzheimer's in a simple, easy-to-understand way, and to keep your mind active with fun, interactive games. Get started on your journey to understand the disease and discover how you can help yourself or someone you love.

Knowledge is the first step.

Source: Alzheimer's Association, 2026 Facts and Figures
